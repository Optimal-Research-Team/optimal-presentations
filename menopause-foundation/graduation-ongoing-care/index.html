      Your hormones don't stand still.

      1. 01
        Before menopauseEstrogen keeps these cells calm.
      2. 02
        After menopauseWith less estrogen, they swell and signal more.

        Hormone therapyEstrogen is replaced, and they quieten.

        Non-hormonal optionA blocker stops the signal getting through.

      Through the year

      When something changes, so does your plan.

      • If hot flashes come back

        Message your care coordinator. Your NP can review your dose before your next visit.

      • If sleep slips

        Your NP asks about sleep at every visit and adjusts your plan.

      • If your mood shifts

        Talk it through at your quarterly visit, or message your care coordinator sooner.

      • If the fog returns

        Reviewed alongside your treatment, with labs when your NP recommends them.

      • If energy dips

        Repeat labs when indicated, to see what's behind it.

      • If muscle starts to slide

        Your quarterly body scan shows it early, so your plan can keep up.

      Before you sign

      Your plan, plainly.

      • Renewal

        Yearly renews automatically. We remind you at least 30 days before.

      • Cancelling

        Yearly: any time before renewal. Monthly: any time; the 3-month minimum still applies.

      • Refunds

        Fees are non-refundable. Your Ontario consumer rights still apply.

      • Unused visits

        They don't carry forward or hold cash value. No-shows may count as used.

      • Extra NP visits

        each, when you or your NP feel one is needed.

      • Rescheduling

        No penalty with over 1 business day's notice. Less notice: once a year.

      • Prescriptions

        Renewals depend on safe monitoring. Your NP may need a visit or bloodwork first.

      • After a gap

        Returning means a new Foundation Program, at then-current rates.

      To cancel, email . Your Ongoing Care Agreement has the full terms.

      Ongoing Care · Menopause & Perimenopause Program
      Before you decide

      Your questions, answered.

      • When does it start?

        Right after your week-12 Foundation visit, with no gap in care.

      • Will I keep my NP?

        Wherever possible, yes, and your full history comes with you either way.

      • What are member events?

        Monthly evenings at the clinic with local speakers, on topics like sleep, strength and bone health.

      • What's a body scan?

        A quick, non-invasive body composition scan at each quarterly visit, to track muscle, fat and changes over time.

      • Are labs included?

        OHIP-covered repeat labs are, whenever your NP recommends them.

      • Is there a minimum commitment?

        Yearly covers 12 months, paid once, and renews unless you cancel before your renewal date. Monthly has a 3-month minimum.

      • Can I use my benefits?

        NP receipts may be claimable on extended health plans or a health spending account.

      • Seen between visits?

        Message your care coordinator. Extra NP visits are each.
