      The moving target

      Your hormones don't stand still.

      As estrogen shifts, so does the brain's temperature switch — a cluster of cells deep in the hypothalamus.

      1. 01
        Before menopauseEstrogen keeps these cells small and calm. Everyday warmth passes unnoticed.
      2. 02
        After menopauseWith little estrogen, the cells swell and signal more often — a small wander tips the switch into a hot flash.
      3. 03
        Hormone therapy, where it suitsEstrogen is replaced; the cells quieten and the switch moves back from its edge.
      4. 04
        A non-hormonal optionThe cells still signal, but a receptor blocker stops the switch receiving it.
