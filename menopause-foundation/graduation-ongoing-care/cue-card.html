Graduation visit — presenter cues

Print & keep at desk · never on screen
Standing rule 1The offer is information, never pressure.
Standing rule 2Bad visit = slow down. Never speed up to the offer.
  1. CongratulationsGive the forest a beat — the sun breaks through as you talk. Reference one thing from her journey before advancing — in her words if you can.
  2. None of this was luckLet the receipt print and stamp, then point at it: "Six steps, every one done — that's not luck." Make the twelve weeks feel heavy — earned, not lucky.
  3. The moving targetLet the 3-D model tour before → after → treatment. One line: "This is why the plan keeps adjusting." Then the road-ahead line verbatim — biology, not pressure. Don't teach endocrinology.
  4. Your choice"Most of our graduates continue with Ongoing Care — here's what both look like." Neutral voice; the layout steers.
  5. Your first stepWalk the year on the dial: Transition Visit, Mid-Year, Annual. Don't book yet — the offer is next.
  6. Your graduate offerVerbatim: "If you decide today or this week, it's $695. If you'd rather take time, it's $795 after — the program is identical either way." Then stop talking. If she says yes, book the Transition Visit now, in the room.
  7. Thank youWarm regardless of decision. Hand over the leave-behind with the deadline written in by hand.
