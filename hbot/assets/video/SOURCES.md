# Micrograph loops

Each loop was generated on Oct 24, 2026 with Kling 3.0 (Higgsfield), mode pro, 5 s,
16:9, no sound. The **same still** was used as both the start and the end image, so
every clip returns to its first frame and loops without a seam. The measured
mean first/last-frame difference is about 1 on a 0–255 scale.

The stills are the beoptimal.ca HBOT page's micrographs (`public/images/hbot/`),
which come from the Optimal design system's v1.3 micrograph layer. Encoded to
1600 px H.264, CRF 25, with no audio track.

All four prompts asked for a locked-off microscope camera, with no zoom, no pan
and no new objects. Each named one gentle, subject-specific motion:
- **telomeres:** filaments shimmer and nuclei pulse;
- **angiogenesis:** light pulses along the vessels;
- **neurons:** signals travel the dendrites;
- **mitochondria:** the mitochondrial network streams.

Cost: about 35 credits for the four.
