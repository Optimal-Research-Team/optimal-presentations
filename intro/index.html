The fine print, plainly

Cancellation & refund policy.

Cancelling annual
$0 to 2 months' fee

Please give us a minimum 60 days' notice prior to your annual renewal date. 0–60 days' notice: $145 per month of late notice.

Refund policy
No refunds

The clinic does not provide refunds for membership fees.

During the notice period, clinicians are not obligated to manage new, non-urgent medical problems. The primary focus is maintaining continuity for existing health concerns during this transition.

A two-way relationship

Your rights and responsibilities.

Your rights
What you can expect of us.
—To receive professional, compassionate care.
—You will be notified of test results that require further clinical management within an appropriate time-frame. We will ask you to book a follow-up appointment if a result requires further discussion.
Your responsibilities
What we expect of you.
—Respectful and courteous behaviour.
—If you are instructed to book a follow-up appointment by your NP, please do so.
—Normal or benign results do not typically result in an immediate appointment or notification. To discuss a specific result, book a follow-up appointment or send us a secure message.

*Please note: except in rare circumstances, the clinic does not typically facilitate switching NPs.
